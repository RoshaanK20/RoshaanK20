# Asset sources

- Character PNGs: generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the user-supplied illustrated portrait. Prompts preserved below. Alpha retained, no automated silhouette or redraw by the SVG builder.
- Fonts: DejaVu Sans Bold (display) and DejaVu Sans Mono (labels), ASCII-plus-punctuation subsets converted to WOFF2 and embedded. See DejaVu-LICENSE.txt. Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/
- Brand icons: Simple Icons release 11.15.0, Python, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, GitHub, LinkedIn and Kaggle. https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons . Individual SVG paths embedded locally. Simple Icons is CC0; brand trademarks remain their owners'.
- Football field, controller and code brackets: original geometric SVG illustrations. These are interest illustrations, not brand marks.

## Generation prompts (built-in image generation)

Portrait: preserve recognizable face, warm skin tone, thick wavy dark hair, beard, black bomber jacket and black crewneck; three-quarter view facing viewer right; chest-up with full head; subtle electric-blue and crimson rim lighting; transparent alpha background; no circle, text or logo.

Pointing character: use generated portrait as identity and style reference; same outfit and face, waist-up, positioned left and pointing toward viewer right with natural index-finger gesture; full head and pointing hand visible; transparent alpha background; no text, arrows or logos.
